01 / Introduction

Following movement across time and place.

An introduction to the problem, the data, and the questions guiding our analysis.

Purpose

Why study migration?

Bird migration connects ecosystems across continents. Understanding its timing and geography can help researchers investigate how species respond to seasonal cycles, changing habitats, and environmental conditions. This project explores whether historical observations and environmental measurements can be used to identify and predict patterns in bird movement.
